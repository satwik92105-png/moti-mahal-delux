Our Story

Heritage & Culinary Excellence

Located in the historic Patna City, Moti Mahal Delux is synonymous with premium dining and grand celebrations. We bring you the authentic taste of North India, crafted with generations-old recipes and the finest ingredients.

From intimate family dinners to grand banquets, our establishment is designed to offer a regal atmosphere. Our signature Tandoori dishes and aromatic Biryanis have made us a beloved culinary landmark in Patna.
